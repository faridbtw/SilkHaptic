- Live log confirms nonzero waveform samples sent over Bluetooth HID.
- Protocol tests passed: independent zlib CRC; first/subsequent report layout;
  four-bit sequence wrap; session counter wrap; stereo separation; sample count;
  high-frequency alias rejection; resampling across fragmented input.
- Final Bluetooth network test delivered and consumed all 28,800 frames, with
  zero queue drops and no disconnect during transmission.
- Physical wireless radio writes sometimes stalled (up to ~87 ms measured).
  The final backend reduces control traffic and keeps recent queued samples
  instead of discarding the entire queue. The final sender transmits no idle
  silence packets, reducing contention with game controller traffic. Wireless timing remains subject to
  radio interference, other controller writers, and macOS scheduling.
- The bridge automatically prefers USB while its audio output is available,
  otherwise uses Bluetooth; no system driver or administrator helper is needed.
- No physical Intel Mac or DualSense Edge was available for validation.
